About Norie

I'm not an agency. I'm the person who actually does the work.

Hi — I'm Norie, a Filipina OFW who spent over 15 years inside corporate HR departments, managing hiring, records, admin systems, and teams across multiple organisations.

So I built NorieDigi to bridge that gap. I combine my HR and process background with modern AI tools to help solopreneurs and small businesses run smoother, look more professional, and grow without burning out.
