About Norie

15 Years of HR Expertise, Now Powering Your Digital Growth

Hi, I'm Norie — a Filipina OFW turned digital entrepreneur with over 15 years of experience in Human Resources and administrative management across corporate environments.

I founded NorieDigi to bridge the gap between traditional business skills and the digital economy — offering AI-powered automation, lead generation, and virtual support services that are practical, affordable, and built to last.

My mission: help you grow your business online without burning out — and prove that OFWs can build thriving digital careers from anywhere in the world.
